Foodza - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Foodza is a nutrition app. It reads food labels, scores products against your own goals and allergies, and keeps a diary of what you eat.

This policy describes exactly what Foodza collects, where it goes, and what it never does. It is written from what the app's code actually does.

The short version

  • You can use Foodza without an account. Scanning, allergen detection, your diary and your profile all work signed out, stored only on your phone.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not advertise. No advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no third party receives your data for their own purposes.
  • We do not track you. Nothing is linked to third-party data for advertising or measurement.
  • Photos you take are never stored on our servers. They are read once and discarded.
  • Apple Health data never leaves your phone.
  • You can delete everything, from inside the app, at any time.

What leaves your phone, and when

If you never sign in

Only three things leave your device: barcodes you scan (sent to Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central to look up the product — no information about you), photographs you choose to use (see Photographs below), and nutrition panels you photograph, added to Foodza's shared food database so the next person who scans that barcode gets an answer.

Your profile, diary and history stay on the phone. A random, non-identifying anonymous identifier is used so a signed-out phone can read and add to the shared food database; it is not shared with anyone and is removed within 90 days of your last use unless tied to a contributed product record.

If you create an account

Signing in is optional and exists so your data survives a lost or replaced phone. When signed in, your email, account identifier, profile, diary, saved products and subscription status are copied to our database, hosted by Supabase in Seoul, South Korea. Row-level security scopes every row to the account that owns it — one user's data is not readable by another, even in principle.

Photographs

A photograph you take is sent to our own server function, passed to Anthropic's Claude model to be read, and discarded once the text or estimate has been extracted. We do not store your photographs. Anthropic does not use the image to train models.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Foodza reads only step count, active energy burned, exercise minutes, and workouts (type, duration, energy) — read only, never written to, and never leaves your phone. Only exercise you type in yourself is backed up.

The AI coach and analysis

Some features send information to Anthropic through our own server, never directly from your phone: the photograph (for label/meal/product reading), or, for the coach, today's logged foods and totals, your goals, preferences and allergies, recent scans and four-week pattern findings. Your name and email are never sent. Anthropic does not use this to train models. Foodza stores the conversation only on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on insight alerts, Foodza may send at most one notification a week, scheduled and computed entirely on your own phone. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Our server verifies Apple's signed receipt and records only which plan you have, whether it is active, when it expires, and Apple's transaction identifier.

Sign-in providers

You can sign in with email and password, Google, or Apple. With Google or Apple sign-in we receive your email address and an identifier only — never your password, contacts, or anything else. If you hide your email with Sign in with Apple, we only see the relay address Apple gives us.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your information to anyone
  • Show you advertising, or share anything with an advertising network
  • Use analytics or attribution SDKs
  • Use your health information for anything other than the features you asked for
  • Build a profile of you for anybody else's benefit

Foodza has no advertising business. Its only revenue is the subscription.

How long we keep things

On your phone: until you delete it, or delete the app. On our servers: for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account cascades through your profile, goals, preferences, allergies, diary, exercise, scans and favourites, with nothing left orphaned. Anonymous product records you contributed remain (facts about a packet of food, not about you) but carry no link back to your account after deletion.

If you are in New Zealand, the Privacy Act 2020 gives you the right to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you and to have it corrected. If you are in the UK or the EU, the UK GDPR and GDPR give you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Write to the address above and we will respond.

Children

Foodza is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. The risk-check questionnaires Foodza links to were validated in adults, so they are not offered to anyone who has told us they are under 18.

Not medical advice

Foodza reports published nutrition guidance and what your own diary says beside it. It does not diagnose, treat or predict disease. Where it links to a validated risk questionnaire, any result you get is a reason to talk to a doctor — not a diagnosis; Foodza never sees your answers or your result. Nothing in the app is a substitute for advice from a doctor, dietitian or allergist. If you have a severe allergy, always check the packaging.

Changes to this policy

If we change what Foodza collects, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.

Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your information, please write to us first. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner in New Zealand (privacy.org.nz), or to your local data protection authority if you are in the UK or the EU.
