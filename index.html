Foodza

Foodza is an iPhone app that scans nutrition labels, barcodes and meals and tells you what it means for you.

Foodza is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

  • Scan labels and barcodesPoint your camera at a nutrition label or barcode and get a plain-words read-out of what is in the product.
  • Scan a mealPhotograph your plate and get an estimate of what is on it, not only packaged food.
  • Checked against your own allergies and goalsTell Foodza your allergies, dietary preferences and goals, and it flags what does not fit, with the reason.
  • Ingredients and additives explainedSee what each ingredient or additive is, with the source of the information named.
  • Healthier alternativesGet suggestions for similar products you could choose instead.
  • A food diary and insightsEvery scan can go into your diary, so you can see patterns over time.
  • Works without an accountScanning, allergen checks, your diary and your profile work signed out and stay on your phone.
